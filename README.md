A one-line, 1-star review written by a houseplant about its human owner,
entered in the [make.fun](https://make.fun/competitions/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-one-star-written-in-sap-2ea1f4f4) bounty "One Star, Written in Sap".

## The entry

> One star: he named me after his ex, so I only get watered when he misses her — I've dropped six leaves spelling it out, and he still thinks I'm just thirsty.

One line, 157 characters — a houseplant's 1-star review of its human owner:
named after the ex, watered only when the owner misses her, and dropping
leaves as hints that keep getting misread as thirst.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the line deterministically
to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
